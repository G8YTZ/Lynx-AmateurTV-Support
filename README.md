# Lynx Amateur TV: Support

Bug reports and ideas for **Lynx Amateur TV**, the amateur television receiver app for Apple TV 4K by G8YTZ.

**Before you post:** Please read the questions and answers at [gb3oo.co.uk/lynx-amateur-tv](https://gb3oo.co.uk/lynx-amateur-tv/). Most setup problems (tuner IP addresses, local network permission, Picotuner control) are answered there.

## Reporting a bug

Click **Issues → New issue → Bug report** and fill in the form. The more detail, the quicker the fix:

- Your Apple TV model and tvOS version (Settings → General → About)
- What you were watching: BATC stream, Picotuner or HDHomeRun
- What happened, and what you expected
- A photo of the screen helps a lot

## Ideas and requests

Use **Issues → New issue → Idea or request**. Not every idea will make it in, but all are read.

## Getting on the air

Amateur television is open to anyone with an amateur radio licence, and the UK Foundation licence is within reach of anyone. Start at [rsgb.org](https://rsgb.org) (getting licensed) and [batc.org.uk](https://batc.org.uk) (amateur TV).
